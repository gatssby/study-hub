- TRANSCRIPTION_ENABLED=false, no real ASR, no automatic activation, no Mac inference.

## Review Focus
- Login expiry and incomplete traversal must not produce removal or empty authoritative catalog.
- New course/year/category must not be blocked by 2026 identifiers or category allowlists.
- Signed URL rotation must not masquerade as content deletion.
- Disk exhaustion and unverified upload must not lose local originals.
- Accidental service start must not start ASR.
